This offseason, LeBron James left the Los Angeles Lakers to explore free agency for the fourth time in his legendary basketball career, with many convinced he would return to one of his former teams, the Miami Heat or the Cleveland Cavaliers.

However, James surprised many by choosing the Philadelphia 76ers, a team that was mentioned as part of the select group of squads that might sign him as he nears retirement. That immediately sparked championship-chasing debates, due to the All-Star players the 76ers already had, including Joel Embiid and Tyrese Maxey.
